Aniimo Version 1.1 Adds Irisalis, Chaos Mode and New Events

The September 23 update adds the Irisalis legendary journey, Egg Heist Chaos Mode, events, quality-of-life changes and bug fixes.

Version 1.1 maintenance was scheduled for September 23 from 00:00 to 10:00 UTC+8, with 500 Glimmer compensation for eligible accounts registered before the update. Players should restart the client after maintenance and verify the current version before reporting an issue.

Official live-service updates

Irisalis enters through Windchaser’s Departure. The event begins September 25 at 10:00 UTC+8 for players who reach Student I; weekly Iris Petals are used to craft its Legendary Aniipod and unlock the Rift Space encounter.

Official live-service updates

Egg Heist Chaos Mode unlocks September 24 at 04:00 UTC+8 after reaching Tier IV Elite Egg Seeker and requires a Chaos Ticket. The update also announces Glamour Star, Aniimo Discovery, Vein Abundance and Journey Chronicles, alongside companion, resource-sharing, voice-to-text, UI, rendering and reconnection fixes.
