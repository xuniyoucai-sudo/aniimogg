Official Beta Reward and Account-Linking Instructions Published

Aniimo explains how eligible test players should use their test email, and when support is required to protect an existing character.

Aniimo’s September 15 notice says eligible previous-test players should sign in to the live game with the same email used during testing. The notice specifically recommends entering that email and verification code rather than using a third-party sign-in if the goal is to claim test rewards.

Official live-service updates

The official guidance distinguishes between accounts before and after character creation. It warns that players who cannot accept losing their existing character, and Steam-test participants with reward issues, should contact in-game Customer Support. Do not follow community account-unlinking suggestions blindly; account actions can affect a character or rewards.
