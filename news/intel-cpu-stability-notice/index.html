Official Intel 13th/14th-Gen Stability Notice Published

Pawprint Studio advises affected desktop Intel users with crashes or out-of-VRAM errors to review BIOS and microcode guidance.

Pawprint Studio says some systems with affected 13th- or 14th-Gen Intel Core desktop processors may experience crashes, freezes, graphical issues, out-of-VRAM errors or blue screens when using outdated microcode. This is not a claim that every Intel PC is affected.

Official live-service updates

The official notice recommends following Intel or motherboard-manufacturer BIOS instructions and using Intel microcode 0x12B or later. Firmware and tuning changes carry risk; users who are not comfortable with them should seek manufacturer or official support rather than copying settings from another player.
