Official PC and Console FAQ Covers Download, Progress and Support

Pawprint Studio documents PC/PS5/Xbox download routes, same-server cross-platform progress and common install guidance.

The official FAQ documents the PC and console service time, Windows PC launcher route, and pre-download paths for PlayStation 5 and Xbox. It asks PC players to reserve at least 40 GB and console players to reserve about 30–35 GB.

Official live-service updates

For accounts, the FAQ says game progress is fully shared across all platforms within the same server, while data on different servers is independent. It also describes Steam-to-Aniimo Pass email linking for previous Steam beta participants. Purchase sharing, server transfer and every platform-specific account workflow are not explained.
