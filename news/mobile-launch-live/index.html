Aniimo Is Officially Live on iOS and Android

Aniimo mobile is now live worldwide with same-server cross-platform progression and official launch rewards.

Pawprint Studio says Aniimo launched worldwide on iOS and Android on September 23. Players should use the official App Store or Google Play listing and avoid APK mirrors or modified clients.

Official live-service updates

The official notice confirms full cross-platform progression when the same account and same server are used. Existing PC or console players can continue their progress, resources and party lineups on mobile; data on different servers remains separate.

Official live-service updates

The launch notice also lists progression, collection, login and pre-registration rewards. Eligibility and delivery should be checked in game, especially when an earlier event or test used a linked email address.
