Aniimo Global Launch Dates Confirmed

PC and consoles launch September 16, followed by mobile September 23.

Pawprint Studio has confirmed a two-wave global launch: PC and consoles on September 16, 2026, followed by iOS and Android on September 23.

Official launch updates

The same announcement opened the Time to Aniimo web event through September 3 at 23:59 UTC+8. Its reward is tied to matching the event email with the email used for the first launch character.
