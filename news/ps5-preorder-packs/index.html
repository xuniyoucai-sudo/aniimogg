PS5 Pre-Order Packs and Prices Verified

The US PS5 store lists Advanced and Collector's reward packs while the base game remains free.

The US PlayStation listing shows an Advanced Edition reward pack at US$9.99 and a Collector's Edition reward pack at US$19.99. Both edition cards include the base-game listing, Fluffy Friend and a version-specific Companion Handbook.

Official launch updates

The base game becomes free at launch. The pre-order period ends September 16 at 09:59 UTC+8. Regional prices and pack availability may differ.
