Official Steam Launch Notice Covers Queues, Rewards and Test Data

Aniimo is live on Steam; the official launch notice flags possible login queues and explains launch-reward and prior-test data boundaries.

Aniimo’s official Steam launch notice says the game is live on Steam and that players may encounter login queues or slower loading during the launch period. It recommends a stable connection, closing unnecessary background programs and checking the published device requirements. These are operational suggestions, not a guarantee that every connection issue has the same cause.

Official live-service updates

The notice says prior test data does not carry into the live game, while eligible test rewards use the official reward process. It also lists launch reward categories, including a Grade I Wayfarer Prismana-form selection, Aniilog collection milestones, event catching items, login-day egg rewards and free outfits. Exact eligibility, timing and regional availability should be checked in-game or through official support. It separately advertises a future PC-and-mobile cross-platform reward, so it should not be claimed before the mobile launch and its terms are available.
