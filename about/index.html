ANIIMO GUIDE

Built by players, for players

Aniimo Guide organizes scattered information into clear, source-aware, versioned guides.

What you can find here

Aniimo.GG is an independent guide to Aniimo. Start with the searchable creature catalogue for names, elements and roles; use the guides for platform, account and gameplay questions. News summaries link back to the announcement they describe.

Who maintains the site

The site is maintained by an independent player-editor under the Aniimo Guide Editorial Team name. It is not an official support service and is not affiliated with the game developer or publisher. We cannot recover game accounts, grant rewards or resolve purchases.

How to judge an answer

Check the source links, information-status label and version note before applying a guide. An official announcement confirms what was announced; it does not mean we personally tested every platform. Beta notes and community reports remain labeled until independently verified.

Corrections and language editions

Send a page URL and supporting source through the contact page when an answer is outdated. English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese pages share the same data; game names may differ between translations. The linked official source is the reference when wording is ambiguous.
