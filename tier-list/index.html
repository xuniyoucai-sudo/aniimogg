Comparison method

A checklist for comparing candidates under consistent conditions. No release-tested character ranking is published here.

Start with a task, not a letter grade

Write down the problem you want to solve: completing a particular encounter, surviving longer or improving an exploration route. A character that helps with one task may not help with another. Compare candidates within the same task before assigning an overall rank.

Build a shortlist from the catalogue

Filter the catalogue by element and role, then open the official Wiki record for each candidate. Treat these labels as a way to find options. A stage, element or role label alone does not establish damage, survivability, availability or investment cost.

Keep comparison conditions consistent

For a useful personal test, record the game version, platform, opponent, character level, upgrades, equipment and team members. Change one candidate at a time where possible. If investment differs, record that difference instead of presenting the result as a direct strength comparison.

Record more than a fastest run

Repeat the same task and note completion time, failures, recovery needs and ease of execution. Keep unsuccessful attempts in your notes. A single favorable result is not enough to establish a reliable recommendation; separate your observation from a general conclusion.

Compare the cost of switching

Before spending limited materials, check the requirements shown in your current game client and what you already own. A small improvement may not justify rebuilding a team. This is a decision checklist, not a claim that any named character needs a particular item or upgrade.

What a future ranking must show

A published ranking needs a defined activity, tested version, comparable investment, recorded observations and limitations. We have not completed that evidence set, so this page does not assign S/A/B grades or claim a best character. Use the linked guides below for the evidence currently available.

Use role-specific comparisons

DPS, BREAK, SUPPORT, HEAL and REGEN answer different needs. Compare candidates within the same job and encounter instead of compressing unlike roles into one score.

Version and evidence policy

Every future recommendation must name the client version and review date, separate official labels from Aniimo.GG testing, and warn when a balance update makes evidence stale. Community reports can suggest a test, but cannot become a rank without repeatable release-build evidence.
