Editorial independence

Advertising will not determine guide conclusions, rankings, source selection, or correction decisions. Advertisers and game companies do not receive advance approval of editorial content. Paid or sponsored material, if introduced, will be clearly labeled at the item level.

Ad placement principles

Ads will be visually distinguishable from navigation, download buttons, filters, and editorial content. We will not ask readers to click ads, disguise ads as site controls, or place ads where accidental clicks are likely. Pages should remain usable with ads blocked or unavailable.

Affiliate and sponsored links

There are currently no affiliate links. If an affiliate or sponsored relationship is introduced, the affected link or article will carry a clear disclosure near the recommendation; ordinary official-source links will not be described as endorsements.
