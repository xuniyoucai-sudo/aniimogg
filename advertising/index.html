ANIIMO GUIDE

Advertising and funding disclosure

Aniimo Guide has applied for Google AdSense, but advertising is not currently active. This page explains what will and will not change if ads are enabled.

Updated · 2026-09-24

Current advertising status

No display ads, paid placements, sponsored articles, or affiliate links are active on this site as of September 24, 2026. An AdSense application or technical review does not mean that Google has approved the site or that ads are being served.

Editorial independence

Advertising will not determine guide conclusions, rankings, source selection, or correction decisions. Advertisers and game companies do not receive advance approval of editorial content. Paid or sponsored material, if introduced, will be clearly labeled at the item level.

Ad placement principles

Ads will be visually distinguishable from navigation, download buttons, filters, and editorial content. We will not ask readers to click ads, disguise ads as site controls, or place ads where accidental clicks are likely. Pages should remain usable with ads blocked or unavailable.

Affiliate and sponsored links

There are currently no affiliate links. If an affiliate or sponsored relationship is introduced, the affected link or article will carry a clear disclosure near the recommendation; ordinary official-source links will not be described as endorsements.

Cookies, consent, and choices

If advertising is enabled, consent controls will be shown where required before optional advertising technologies are used. Data handling, browser storage, Google partner-site information, and reader choices are described in the Privacy Policy.

Report an advertising problem

Use the contact page to report an ad that obscures content, imitates a control, is inappropriate, or creates a performance or accessibility problem. Include the page URL, approximate time, country or region, and a screenshot that contains no personal information.
