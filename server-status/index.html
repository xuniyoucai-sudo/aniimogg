Before and after maintenance — editorial checklist

  1. Finish time-sensitive actions before planned downtime and preserve your account/server details.
  2. The official notice says login is unavailable during maintenance. Reinstalling or relinking is not a remedy for planned downtime.
  3. After official completion, restart the game client for the update. If access still fails, report platform, server, build, timestamp and the exact error to official support.

FAQ

Does 11:00 UTC guarantee service is restored?

No. It is the planned end and may change; check official completion information and your client.

Are the regional windows simultaneous?

Yes. The stated UTC+0, UTC-4 and UTC+8 windows all convert to 06:00–11:00 UTC.

Is this a live status monitor?

No. It is a source-linked schedule. No live health API or game login was tested.
