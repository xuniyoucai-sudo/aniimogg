Gift code reports

These are third-party reports, not Aniimo.GG in-game redemption tests. Source checks are dated September 28, 2026; current eligibility and expiry are unknown unless specifically sourced.

Verification boundary

Codes can expire, reach a claim limit or become account-ineligible without notice. A reported-active label is not an official guarantee. Never provide a password, verification code or payment to claim a gift code.

How to redeem

Open the phone menu, choose Settings → Account → Gift Code Redemption, enter the case-sensitive code, then claim the reward from in-game mail. The mailbox unlocks after progressing the Chasing the Clouds story.
