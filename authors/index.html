Aniimo Guide Editorial Team

The editorial team researches official announcements, records sources, and reviews guides for clarity and version accuracy.

Testing standards

Release-version gameplay claims will be checked in game and labeled with platform, version, and verification date whenever those details matter.

Source and review workflow

We prefer first-party patch notes, store listings, official support pages, and reproducible public-build checks. Beta history and community reports are labeled separately, translations are checked against the source, and no ranking is called tested without comparable evidence.

Editorial independence

Coverage, corrections, and guide conclusions are not sold to publishers or advertisers. Advertising, if approved and enabled, will be separated from editorial content and will not change review conclusions.

Corrections

Confirmed errors are updated promptly. Readers can submit evidence through the public issue tracker on the contact page.
