What is Aniimo?

Aniimo is a free-to-play, open-world creature-catching RPG from Pawprint Studio. Players explore the continent of Idyll, catch creatures with Aniipods, and use Twine to become collected Aniimo for combat, puzzles and traversal. Official store information confirms single-player, online co-op, online PvP and cross-platform multiplayer, while detailed launch-version systems will be verified before they are treated as settled facts.

When does Aniimo release?

Aniimo launches globally on PC and consoles on September 16, 2026, followed by iOS and Android on September 23. PlayStation lists 10:00 UTC+8 for PS5, but exact Steam, Epic Games, Xbox and mobile unlock hours have not been separately confirmed. Our release and server-time guides distinguish official dates from estimates and local store displays.

Is Aniimo crossplay?

Yes. Steam officially lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer alongside online co-op and online PvP. Pawprint Studio has not yet published the exact platform pairings or confirmed cross-save, cross-progression and shared-purchase rules. The crossplay guide keeps confirmed multiplayer support separate from account and progress features that still require an official launch FAQ.

FAQ

Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes. Pawprint Studio and the official platform listings describe the base game as free to play, with optional in-game purchases.

What platforms is Aniimo on?

The announced platforms are PlayStation 5, Xbox, Steam, Epic Games Store, iOS and Android.

How many Aniimo are confirmed?

The public official Wiki listed 94 visible index entries when checked on August 30, 2026.
