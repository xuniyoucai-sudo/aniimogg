These checks begin when the public build is available. Results will record platform, build, region, time and reproducible steps.

Pending public-build verification

  1. ○Use only an official store entry; sign in to the platform account you plan to keep
  2. ○Record platform, region, build number, install size and first update before testing
  3. ○Confirm the live download or preload state; do not treat a paid pack as proof of client access
  4. ○Login, queues, regions and maintenance messages
  5. ○Controller prompts and keyboard rebinding
  6. ○Crossplay, parties and account-link behavior
  7. ○Accessibility menu and readable defaults
  8. ○Frame pacing, graphics presets and crash recovery
  9. ○Code redemption path and regional eligibility
  10. ○Server names, latency display and reconnect flow
