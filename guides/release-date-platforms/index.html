Aniimo Release Date and Platforms

Aniimo releases on PC, PS5 and Xbox September 16, 2026, followed by iOS and Android September 23. Check the confirmed time and platform status.

Release dates at a glance

Pawprint Studio announced September 16, 2026 for PC and consoles, followed by September 23 for iOS and Android. The PlayStation listing specifies 10:00 UTC+8 on September 16; exact Steam, Epic, Xbox and mobile unlock hours have not been separately confirmed.

Confirmed platforms

The official site lists PlayStation 5, Xbox, Steam, Epic Games Store, App Store, and Google Play. No PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch or Mac version has been officially announced.

Why Steam may show September 15

Steam displays September 15 in some regions. A September 16 launch in UTC+8 falls on September 15 in western time zones, but this does not prove every platform unlocks simultaneously. Follow the local store countdown.

Cross-platform support

Steam lists cross-platform multiplayer alongside single-player, online co-op and online PvP. Final account-linking and cross-progression rules remain unconfirmed.

Release checklist

Wishlist or pre-register on your preferred platform, reserve at least 45 GB on PC, and check the official news page for preload and server-opening notices.
