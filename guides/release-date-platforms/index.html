Aniimo Release Date and Platforms

Aniimo launches on PC and consoles September 16, 2026, followed by mobile September 23. See confirmed platforms and timing notes.

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-27

Official global release dates

Pawprint Studio announced September 16, 2026 for PC and consoles, followed by September 23 for iOS and Android. The PlayStation listing specifies 10:00 UTC+8 on September 16.

Confirmed platforms

The official site lists PlayStation 5, Xbox, Steam, Epic Games Store, App Store, and Google Play. No PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, or Mac version has been officially announced.

Why Steam may show September 15

Steam currently displays September 15 in some regions. This is consistent with a September 16 launch in UTC+8 appearing on the previous calendar day in western time zones; follow the store countdown for your local unlock.

Cross-platform support

Steam lists cross-platform multiplayer alongside single-player, online co-op, and online PvP. Final account-linking and cross-progression rules should be confirmed at launch.

Release checklist

Wishlist or pre-register on your preferred platform, reserve at least 45 GB on PC, and check the official news page for preload and server-opening notices.
