Confirmed Aniimo Characters

A verified overview of officially revealed Aniimo, including Emberpup, Celestis, Nimbi, Bolty and evolution lines.

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-27

The official index is live

The official Aniimo Wiki currently lists more than 90 numbered entries. These are confirmed creatures rather than names inferred from leaks or fan art.

Early official examples

Emberpup begins the official index at No.001, followed by Flameruff, Scorchhowl, and Inferlupa. Celestis and Stellarys appear at No.005–006, while Nimbi is No.018 and Bolty is No.048.

Elements and roles

The official index filters creatures by elements including holy, fire, ice, dark, electric, grass, water, rock, and wind, plus roles such as DPS, Heal, Support, Break, and Regen.

Forms and evolution

Official entries can include stages, alternate forms, habitats, mobility, traits, and skills. For example, Emberpup has multiple forms and an evolution line, while Nimbi is presented as a wind/electric support Aniimo.

Why this is not a tier list

Confirmation does not establish strength. Stats and skills can change before or after launch, so rankings will remain separate and noindex until repeatable release-version testing is complete.
