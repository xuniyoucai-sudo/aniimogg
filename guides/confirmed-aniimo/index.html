Confirmed Aniimo Characters

A verified overview of officially revealed Aniimo, including Emberpup, Celestis, Nimbi, Bolty and evolution lines.

The official index is live

The public official Aniimo Wiki listed 94 visible entries when checked on August 30, 2026. These are confirmed index records rather than names inferred from leaks or fan art.

Early official examples

Emberpup begins the official index at No.001, followed by Flameruff, Scorchhowl, and Inferlupa. Celestis and Stellarys appear at No.005–006, while Nimbi is No.018 and Bolty is No.048.

Elements and roles

The official index uses nine element filters: holy, fire, ice, dark, electric, grass, water, rock, and wind. Its five role filters are DPS, Heal, Support, Break, and Regen.

How to use the compendium

Browse the card gallery or switch to a compact table. Search and filters work in both views; use the bookmark on a card to keep a private collection on this device. Every entry links to its official Wiki record and shows only the stage, elements and role returned by the official index.

Why this is not a tier list

Confirmation does not establish strength. Stats and skills can change before or after launch, so rankings remain separate until repeatable release-version testing is complete.
