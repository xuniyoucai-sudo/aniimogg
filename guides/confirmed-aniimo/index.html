What this Aniimo list includes

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

130 official form records: 118 Basic + 12 Prismana. Public index snapshot: 2026-11-06. This is not a species, obtainable-form or owned count; no in-game test is implied.

Early official examples

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Emberpup begins the official index at No.001, followed by Flameruff, Scorchhowl, and Inferlupa. Celestis and Stellarys appear at No.005–006, while Nimbi is No.017 and Bolty is No.045.

Elements and roles

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The verified English Wiki root displays Light, Fire, Ice, Dark, Lightning, Grass, Water, Rock and Wind. Light and Lightning retain the internal keys holy and electric. Older pages may still say Holy or Electric; these are display differences, not evidence of new elements or balance changes. Roles remain DPS, Heal, Support, Break and Regen.

How to use the compendium

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Browse the card gallery or switch to a compact table. Search and filters work in both views; use the bookmark on a card to keep a private collection on this device. Every entry links to its official Wiki record and shows only the stage, elements and role returned by the official index.

Read stages without over-interpreting them

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Lumin, Gamma and Nova are index stage labels. They are useful for filtering the official catalogue, but they do not by themselves state an evolution route, required level, item, location, or the strength of an Aniimo. Open the official entry and the Evolution and Forms guide when you need those boundaries.

Build a private checklist, not a public claim

Aniimo.GG editorial guidance

Bookmarks are stored only in this browser. They are useful for tracking the Aniimo you want to investigate, but they do not sync to the game, verify ownership, or represent a game account collection.

Why this is not a tier list

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Confirmation does not establish strength. Stats and skills can change before or after launch, so rankings remain separate until repeatable release-version testing is complete.

FAQ

How many form records are in this snapshot?

130 official form records: 118 Basic + 12 Prismana. Public index snapshot: 2026-11-06. This is not a species, obtainable-form or owned count; no in-game test is implied.

Can I filter Aniimo by element, role or stage?

Yes. The gallery and table share the same name, number, element, role and stage filters.

Do the stages reveal evolution requirements?

No. A stage is an official index label, not a confirmed evolution recipe or requirement.

Does a role or element make an Aniimo high tier?

No. These labels describe the official index taxonomy. They are not a ranking or a release-tested build recommendation.

Do saved Aniimo sync with my game account?

No. Bookmarks are saved only in this browser and are not connected to a game account.

Why are there two No.001 records?

Numbering is regional. Select the group and verify the name and official path: Emberpup and Reefish are different records. Old bookmarks are retained and new regional entries have separate keys.

Does the count of 130 mean 130 unique Aniimo species?

No. It is the current visible form-record total: 118 Basic and 12 Prismana records. Names can repeat across forms and groups.

Does code 4 mean Nova or a new evolution stage?

No verified name is available in the checked public pages, which show stagesMap.4. The site preserves the code without assigning a meaning.

Were 46 new Aniimo added on November 5?

No. The visible total remains 118. Forty-six existing records have different stage labels in the checked Wiki snapshot; this does not prove a gameplay change.
