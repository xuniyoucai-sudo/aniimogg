Aniimo Evolution and Forms

How Aniimo evolution stages, branching evolution and alternate forms are presented in official Wiki and beta information.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-02Information versionOfficial Wiki / Beta evolution timelineInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-02

Official stages

The official Wiki filters entries by Lumin, Gamma and Nova stages. Evolution lines can contain several numbered entries or more than one Nova outcome, so stage and species ID must be tracked separately.

Branching evolution and player choice

Official beta material describes multi-branch evolution. A January 30 beta update said Fenrier unlocked the option to evolve into Glynsera at level 29, showing that a condition may open a choice rather than force an immediate evolution.

Location and bonding conditions

A February 4 update improved evolution-condition wording and added coordinate tracking for objectives at specific locations. It also changed Piopiota's condition to approaching and successfully bonding with Susuta until it turns red and follows the player.

Forms and Morph Orbs

The same update added Basic forms to Morph Orb transformation options. Forms may change appearance, element, skills or movement without necessarily becoming a different species entry; Morph Orb availability and costs still need launch verification.

Special form terms

Beta notices mention Prismana, Darkler, Sparkling, Alpha and weather-related forms. Event preservation and trading rules varied, so none should be generalized to every form at launch.

Release database plan

The release-tested database will separate species ID, stage, form ID, unlock objective, element, skills, location, item cost and game version so beta conditions are not presented as permanent rules.

FAQ

Does every Aniimo evolve at a fixed level?

No. Official beta examples include levels, branching choices, bonding interactions and location objectives.

How did Fenrier evolve into Glynsera in beta?

A January 30 update said the option unlocked when Fenrier reached level 29. The launch condition is unconfirmed.

What was Piopiota's beta evolution condition?

A February 4 update required approaching and bonding with Susuta until it turned red and followed the player.

Are forms the same as evolution stages?

Not always. Basic, habitat, weather and special forms can be transformations rather than a new evolution-stage species.

Are these final launch conditions?

No. They are versioned beta examples that will be rechecked in the public build.
