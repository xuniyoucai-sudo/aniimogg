Aniimo Evolution and Forms

How Aniimo evolution stages, branching evolution and alternate forms are presented in official Wiki and beta information.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-28Information versionOfficial Wiki / BetaInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Official stages

The official Wiki filters entries by Lumin, Gamma, and Nova stages. Individual evolution lines can contain multiple numbered creatures or more than one Nova outcome.

Branching evolution

Official beta material describes multi-branch evolution. A beta update also noted that Fenrier could unlock the option to evolve into Glynsera at level 29, showing that evolution can involve conditions and player choice.

Forms are not always evolutions

Official entries may list Basic, habitat-related, weather-related, or other forms. Forms can change element, appearance, skills, or movement without necessarily becoming a different index number.

Special form terms

Official beta notices mention Prismana, Darkler, Sparkling, Alpha, weather forms, and other variants. Availability and preservation rules differed across events and should not be generalized to launch.

What the database will record

After release we will separate species ID, evolution stage, form ID, unlock condition, element, skills, habitat, and version so forms are not mistaken for entirely separate species.
