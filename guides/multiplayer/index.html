Aniimo Multiplayer Explained

Confirmed Aniimo multiplayer features including online co-op, PvP, cross-platform play, camps and three-player Egg Heist.

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-27

Confirmed online modes

Steam lists single-player, online co-op, online PvP, and cross-platform multiplayer. The store also discloses in-game chat and online interaction.

Three-player Egg Heist

Egg Heist is described as a PvEvP mode in the Lost Isles. Players join a three-person team, search for supplies or objectives, fight in real time, and compete around a Giant Darkler Egg.

Open-world social spaces

Each Pathfinder has a personal RV at a campsite where players can meet and interact. Homeland activities include planting crops, building decorations, and arranging a personal space with Aniimo.

Solo play remains supported

Official descriptions explicitly mention solo and multiplayer play. That does not yet confirm which story activities allow drop-in co-op or how world progress is credited to guests.

Cross-progression is still unclear

Cross-platform multiplayer is listed, but final cross-save, account linking, regional server, friend-limit, voice-chat, and platform-subscription requirements need launch confirmation.
