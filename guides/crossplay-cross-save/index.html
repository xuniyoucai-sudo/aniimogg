Is Aniimo Crossplay? Cross-Platform and Cross-Save Status

Is Aniimo crossplay? See the confirmed cross-platform multiplayer listing and the current cross-save, cross-progression and account-linking status.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-16Information versionOfficial PC and console pre-download FAQInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-16

Last reviewed · 2026-09-16

Official sources rechecked September 16, 2026. The newly published PC and console FAQ is reflected below; details the FAQ does not address remain explicitly marked.

Short answer

Yes. Steam lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer, online co-op and online PvP. In addition, Pawprint Studio’s September 14 FAQ says game progress is fully shared across all platforms within the same server.

What the official FAQ confirms

The FAQ explicitly distinguishes servers: data on different servers is independent, but progress is shared across all platforms inside the same server. This is the current official basis for cross-progression.

What it does not yet explain

The FAQ does not publish a platform-pair matrix, full account-linking steps for every identity provider, purchase-sharing rules, character-transfer rules between servers, or regional restrictions. Those details must not be inferred.

Steam account linking

For previous Steam beta participants, the FAQ says a Steam account can be linked to an Aniimo Pass email account through the Steam client prompt or the in-game Email Link page. Other platform-specific linking steps are not detailed.

Safe setup advice

Use one recoverable email where the platform permits it, choose the intended server carefully, and do not assume that purchases or characters can move between separate servers.

Official FAQ confirmation

The official September 14 PC and console FAQ says that game progress is fully shared across all platforms within the same server, while data on different servers is independent. This is an explicit progress-sharing statement, not an inference from a store feature label.

FAQ

Is Aniimo crossplay?

Yes. Steam lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer, online co-op and online PvP.

Does Aniimo have cross-save or shared progress?

The official September 14 FAQ says progress is fully shared across all platforms within the same server. Data on different servers is independent.

Can I move a character between servers?

That is not confirmed. The FAQ only says data on different servers is independent.

How do I link a Steam account?

The FAQ says previous Steam beta users can link Steam to an Aniimo Pass email through the Steam prompt or the in-game Email Link page. Other platform workflows are not detailed.
