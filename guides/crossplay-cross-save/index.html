Is Aniimo Crossplay? Cross-Platform and Cross-Save Status

Is Aniimo crossplay? See the confirmed cross-platform multiplayer listing and the current cross-save, cross-progression and account-linking status.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-31Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-31

Short answer

Yes, Steam officially lists Aniimo as supporting Cross-Platform Multiplayer, online co-op and online PvP. The exact PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox and mobile combinations have not yet been documented, so only the general crossplay feature is confirmed.

Crossplay, cross-save and cross-progression are different

Crossplay lets people on different platforms play together. Cross-save or cross-progression carries characters and progress between platforms. Pawprint Studio has not yet confirmed cross-save, shared purchases or character transfer.

Account linking remains unconfirmed

Launch instructions for linking Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation, Xbox, Apple and Google identities have not been published. Pre-registration with an email account does not by itself prove that every platform identity will merge.

Purchases, regions and friend lists

Premium currency, platform-exclusive items, server regions and friend lists may have restrictions. Do not buy currency on one platform expecting it to appear elsewhere until an official launch FAQ confirms the rules.

Safest launch approach

Choose a primary platform, use the same recoverable email where the platform permits it, and avoid creating duplicate characters until official account-linking instructions are available.

Confirmed event rule versus cross-save

The official Time to Aniimo web event says its reward reaches the first launch character when the event email matches the email used to create that character. This confirms reward attribution for one event only; it does not confirm cross-save, platform-account merging or progress transfer.

FAQ

Is Aniimo crossplay?

Yes. Steam lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer, but the exact supported platform combinations have not yet been published.

Does Aniimo support cross-platform multiplayer?

Cross-platform multiplayer is officially listed together with online co-op and online PvP. Final pairing and server details remain pending.

Does Aniimo have cross-save?

Cross-save and cross-progression have not been officially confirmed. Do not assume characters, progress or purchases transfer between platforms.

Can I move progress between PC, console and mobile?

Pawprint Studio has not published account-linking or progress-transfer rules for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox, iOS and Android.
