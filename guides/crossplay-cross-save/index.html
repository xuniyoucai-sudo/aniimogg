Aniimo Crossplay and Cross-Save

What is confirmed about Aniimo cross-platform multiplayer, and what remains unknown about cross-save and account linking.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-28Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Crossplay is officially listed

Steam lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer alongside online co-op and online PvP. This supports play between more than one platform, although the exact platform combinations are not yet documented.

Cross-save is not confirmed

Pawprint Studio has not published final cross-progression rules. Crossplay does not automatically mean that saves, purchases, or characters transfer between PC, console, and mobile.

Account linking questions

The official site uses account-based registration, but launch rules for linking Steam, Epic, PlayStation, Xbox, Apple, and Google identities are still pending.

Purchases and regional servers

Premium currency, platform-exclusive items, server regions, and friend lists may have platform restrictions. Wait for the launch FAQ before purchasing with the expectation of transfer.

Safe launch approach

Choose one primary platform, use the same recoverable email where possible, and avoid creating duplicate characters until official linking instructions are available.
