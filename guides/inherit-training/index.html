Inherit Training Transfer Guide

Compare Aniimo Inherit with the announced Whirlbite reset: eligible Basic/Prismana individuals, refund effects, regional November 12–27 windows and confirmation risks.

Prepared by the independent player-editor · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-11-11

Whirlbite reset: announced, not independently tested

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The November 9 official update preview lists Whirlbite Basic Form and Prismana Form obtained before this balance adjustment as eligible for a limited reset. It returns named resources and removes corresponding training; it is not Inherit, which moves a training profile to another individual. Later catches and other forms are not confirmed eligible by this wording.

Regional windows are not a promise of early access

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The reset is listed for November 12–27 at 04:00 in each server offset shown below. These are different global moments. On November 12, maintenance is Europe 06:00–11:00 UTC+0, Americas 02:00–07:00 UTC-4 and Apac 14:00–19:00 UTC+8. The listed 04:00 reset start is before maintenance in Europe/Apac, but inside the Americas maintenance window. The notice does not resolve access before the update; maintenance can vary. Do not assume the reset menu is usable at the listed start or while login is unavailable.

Before accepting a reset — editorial checks

Aniimo.GG editorial guidance

Record the exact individual, form and training panels, then compare eligibility and every refund in the current preview after the official update. Do not inherit, release or retrain merely to test qualification. Stop and ask support if the preview differs. The notice does not separately specify every Ascend, Held Item or skill investment, prior-Inherit interaction, menu path, use limit or reversal guarantee. No real reset was performed for this guide.

What Inherit is for

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Inherit is a training-management tool for moving an invested profile onto a more suitable Aniimo instead of rebuilding it from zero. Official material confirms automatic Capafruit transfer. The broader workflow below comes from version-labelled public-build 1.0 interface records and should be rechecked after updates.

Identity and training are separate

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Community public-build records show that both Aniimo remain in the Collection: the transfer changes their training profiles rather than consuming the source. Species, form, personality and other identity traits stay with each Aniimo; levels and named training progress move according to the preview.

Read the transfer direction before confirming

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The recorded interface places the trained source on the left and the intended recipient on the right. Do not rely on memory or a guide screenshot: compare both names, levels, Resonance and Potential panels in the current confirmation screen before accepting.

Check eligibility before planning a transfer

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Version 1.0 records normally limit Inherit to the same evolution family and may reject Aniimo assigned to combat or exploration formations. Basic Irisalis is reported as a source-side family exception. These are community-observed client rules, not permanent official guarantees.

What the versioned client record says moves

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The public-build help record lists EXP and level, Resonance stage and star rank, Capability Awakening, Capafruit effects and other training progress as one package, with surplus training items returned. Exact fields, refund handling and eligibility should be confirmed in the live preview.

A safe six-step workflow

Aniimo.GG editorial guidance

First compare the exact form, Innate Potential, personality and skill fit. Remove both Aniimo from active formations if required. Put the trained profile on the source side, select the intended recipient, read every before-and-after value, then review the displayed fee and confirm only when the direction is correct.

When Inherit is useful

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Use it when a clearly better same-family recipient matches the role you need and the existing training investment is worth preserving. It is a safety net for roster upgrades, not evidence that every new copy deserves an immediate transfer.

What this guide does not freeze as fact

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The current fee, every selection restriction and the Irisalis exception are client-version details. Aniimo.GG does not present a third-party screenshot, one account result or an old numeric cost as a permanent official rule; the current confirmation screen takes priority.

FAQ

Is the Whirlbite reset the same as Inherit?

No. The limited reset refunds named resources and removes their training effects; Inherit transfers a training profile between individuals.

Can every Whirlbite reset at the advertised 04:00 start?

The notice names pre-adjustment Basic/Prismana individuals, but does not explain every prior-Inherit case or establish a usable menu before the update. Check the live preview and official guidance.

Does Inherit delete the source Aniimo?

Version-labelled public-build records show both Aniimo remain in the Collection and exchange training profiles; verify the current preview before confirming.

What does Inherit transfer?

Community client records list level and EXP, Resonance, Capability Awakening, Capafruit and other training progress. The official developer letter specifically confirms automatic Capafruit transfer.

Can any two Aniimo use Inherit?

Public-build 1.0 records normally require the same family, with a reported source-side exception for basic Irisalis. Treat the current selector as authoritative.

Does personality or form transfer?

They are identity traits, not the training package. Compare the exact before-and-after preview so you do not confuse the recipient's identity with the incoming training.

Should I trust a fixed Inherit cost from a guide?

No. Fees and restrictions are version-dependent; use the amount shown in the live confirmation screen.
