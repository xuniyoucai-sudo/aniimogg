Catching, Twine & Exploration

Learn Aniimo catching, Twine, exploration, multiplayer and the announced post-launch NVIDIA ACE companion interactions.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-09-03Information versionPre-launch / announced post-launch NVIDIA ACE updateInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-03

Catch with timing and tools

Official descriptions say players use Aniipods, set traps and time a catch. Which Aniimo appear can depend on environment, weather, phenomena and time of day; exact release rates and optimal tools still need testing.

Twine for combat, puzzles and movement

Twine lets a Pathfinder become a collected Aniimo and use its perspective and abilities. Official examples include solving puzzles, fighting, gliding, diving and burrowing rather than treating every Aniimo as only a battle unit.

Evolution and forms

Most Aniimo can evolve, and official index entries include different forms. Evolution conditions, form availability and stat changes should be checked against the public release before beta instructions are treated as final.

Solo, multiplayer and real-time challenges

Steam lists single-player, online co-op, online PvP and cross-platform multiplayer. Egg Heist is the clearest confirmed three-player PvEvP example, while general story co-op limits remain unpublished.

RV camps and Homeland

Each Pathfinder has a personal RV at an open-world campsite where players can meet. Homeland is described as supporting crops, decoration and arranging a space with Aniimo, but launch limits and visitor permissions still require verification.

NVIDIA ACE companion interactions are a post-launch update

Pawprint Studio announced a future NVIDIA ACE update for voice and natural-language conversations with Aniimo. Players will be able to chat with companions, teach them new skills, send them to gather resources and see dynamic emotional responses. The announcement does not promise these features for the September 16 launch build or confirm every language, platform or Aniimo.

What NVIDIA ACE does not confirm

This companion feature is separate from player voice chat, speech-to-text accessibility and cloud gaming. GeForce NOW support, offline processing, microphone privacy settings, supported languages and a release date remain unconfirmed.

FAQ

Will Aniimo support voice conversations with creatures?

Pawprint Studio has announced voice and natural-language companion interactions powered by NVIDIA ACE for a post-launch update.

Is NVIDIA ACE available at launch?

The announcement calls it a post-launch update and gives no release date, so it should not be treated as a September 16 launch feature.

Does NVIDIA ACE mean Aniimo supports GeForce NOW?

No. NVIDIA ACE is an interaction technology announcement, not confirmation of cloud-streaming availability.
