Catching, Twine & Exploration

Learn the confirmed core gameplay systems in Aniimo.

Catch and train

Meet and catch Aniimo throughout the open world, then train them for combat and exploration.

Twine

Twine lets the player merge with an Aniimo and use a different perspective or ability to move through the world.

Explore together

Official material highlights gliding, diving, burrowing, challenges, racing, solo play and multiplayer exploration.
