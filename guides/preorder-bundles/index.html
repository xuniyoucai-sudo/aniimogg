Aniimo Pre-Order Packs Compared

Compare Aniimo's PS5 Advanced and Collector's pre-order packs, prices, Companion Handbooks, exclusive reward and free base game.

Published2026-09-01Updated2026-09-01Information versionPre-launch PlayStation listingInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-01

The base game is free

Aniimo launches as a free-to-play game. The PlayStation pre-order prices buy optional reward packs, not access to the base game. Players can wait for launch and download the base game without buying either edition.

Advanced Edition — US$9.99 on PS5

The Advanced pack includes the free base-game listing, one PlayStation-exclusive Fluffy Friend accessory and one Advanced Companion Handbook. The handbook includes a version-dependent Prismana Aniimo Egg with Perfect Potential, a related branching-evolution material and other progression rewards unlocked as the handbook levels.

Collector's Edition — US$19.99 on PS5

The Collector's listing includes the free base-game listing, the same Fluffy Friend accessory and a Collector's Companion Handbook. PlayStation does not provide a complete item-by-item comparison on the main product page, so this guide does not invent additional Collector rewards.

Important handbook conditions

The handbook is an in-game item that remains active until the end of the current version after redemption. Its exclusive Prismana Egg can vary by version, so the actual reward depends on when it is redeemed.

Deadline and regional pricing

The PlayStation pre-order period ends September 16, 2026 at 09:59 UTC+8, one minute before the listed PS5 launch. US prices are US$9.99 and US$19.99; local prices, taxes and refund rules can differ by PlayStation region.

Should you pre-order?

Buy only if the listed cosmetic and handbook rewards are worth the local price to you. A purchase is not required to play, and the current listing does not establish the long-term value of handbook progression or version-dependent eggs.

FAQ

Is Aniimo free even though PlayStation sells pre-order editions?

Yes. The base game becomes free at launch; the paid editions are optional reward packs.

What is in the Advanced Edition?

The US PS5 listing confirms Fluffy Friend x1 and Advanced Companion Handbook x1, with a version-dependent Prismana Egg, evolution material and other handbook rewards.

What is in the Collector's Edition?

The main US PS5 edition list confirms Fluffy Friend x1 and Collector's Companion Handbook x1. It does not show a complete item-by-item Collector reward breakdown.

When does Aniimo pre-order end?

PlayStation states September 16, 2026 at 09:59 UTC+8.

Are these prices available on every platform?

No. The comparison uses the US PS5 listing. Platform availability and regional prices may differ.
