Aniimo Server Launch Time by Region

Aniimo global launch dates and the currently confirmed server-opening time converted for major regions.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-15Information versionOfficial Steam regional launch-time announcementInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-15

Last reviewed · 2026-09-11

Official sources rechecked September 11, 2026. No additional platform-specific details were published; unconfirmed items below remain explicitly marked.

Confirmed launch time

Steam’s September 15 official announcement confirms PC and console launch at September 16, 2026, 10:00 UTC+8. This matches the PlayStation listing. The announcement separately lists America, APAC and Europe times; it does not publish a separate Xbox unlock rule.

Regional times listed by Steam

Steam lists Aniimo-America at September 15, 22:00 UTC-4; Aniimo-APAC at September 16, 10:00 UTC+8; and Aniimo-Europe at September 16, 02:00 UTC+0. These three times represent the same global moment.

Mobile launch time

September 23 is confirmed for mobile, but a precise global server-opening hour has not been published. Do not assume it uses the same 10:00 UTC+8 time until Pawprint Studio confirms it.

Steam date difference

Steam’s America time is September 15 because it is the same global launch moment as September 16 in UTC+8. This confirms the listed Steam regions, but does not establish an Epic, Xbox or mobile store unlock time.

Server details still to verify

The Steam notice names America, APAC and Europe but does not settle final server labels in game, maintenance windows, character-transfer rules or account-region restrictions. Those require public-build verification.
