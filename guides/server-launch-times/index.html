Aniimo Server Launch Time by Region

Aniimo global launch dates and the currently confirmed server-opening time converted for major regions.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-28Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Confirmed launch time

The official PlayStation listing states that the free-to-play launch begins September 16, 2026 at 10:00 UTC+8. Pawprint Studio separately confirms September 16 for PC and consoles and September 23 for mobile.

Regional conversion

10:00 UTC+8 equals 02:00 UTC, 03:00 BST, 04:00 CEST, 22:00 EDT on September 15, and 19:00 PDT on September 15. Daylight-saving rules can change, so confirm your local store countdown.

Mobile launch time

September 23 is confirmed for mobile, but a precise global server-opening hour has not been published. Do not assume it uses the same 10:00 UTC+8 time until Pawprint Studio confirms it.

Steam date difference

Steam may display September 15 in western regions because the same global moment falls on the previous local calendar day.

Server regions

Beta notices used APAC, Europe, and America schedules, but final launch server names, maintenance windows, and character-transfer rules remain unconfirmed.
