Aniimo Languages and Voice Acting

Aniimo language support on Steam, including interface, subtitles and currently listed full-audio languages.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-31Information versionPre-launch Steam listingInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-31

Thirteen Steam languages

Steam currently lists English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, French, German, Russian, Indonesian, Thai, Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish, and Vietnamese interface and subtitle support.

Four full-audio languages on Steam

As checked on August 31, Steam marks English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean for full audio. The remaining nine listed languages currently show interface and subtitles without full audio.

Text and subtitle support

All thirteen listed languages currently have interface and subtitle check marks, making them the safest confirmed localization set for the PC release.

Console and mobile differences

This table confirms the current Steam PC listing only. Language packs and voice options may differ by platform or region, so check the local console or mobile store before downloading if a specific language is essential.

Post-launch updates

We will verify language selection, downloadable voice packs, subtitle sizing, dubbing credits, and whether voice settings can be changed without reinstalling.
