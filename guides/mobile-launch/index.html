Aniimo Mobile Launch and Download Guide

Aniimo mobile is live on iOS and Android. Get the official iOS and Android links, account-sharing details, storage guidance and confirmed mobile performance notes.

Published2026-09-19Updated2026-09-23Information versionOfficial mobile launch, account and device FAQInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-23

Mobile launch and pre-download time

Official Aniimo support says pre-download began September 22, 2026 at 00:00 UTC+8 and the iOS and Android version launches September 23 at 10:00 UTC+8. The same launch moment is September 22 at 22:00 UTC-4 in America and September 23 at 02:00 UTC in Europe. Store availability can still vary by local region.

Use official store links only

Download from the official Google Play listing or official App Store listing. Aniimo says to reserve at least 18 GB of free storage and use stable Wi-Fi for the download. Do not use APK mirrors, altered clients, account sellers or unofficial launchers.

Continue with the right account and server

Official guidance says progress is fully shared across platforms on the same server, while different servers keep separate progress. On a first third-party-platform sign-in, use Link Email and use the same linked email from pre-registration, tests or official web events when applicable; the notice says skipping that link can prevent associated rewards or progress from being received.

Officially confirmed mobile performance notes

Aniimo says the mobile client supports 60 FPS except on a small number of lower-end devices. Higher frame rate may increase heat, and the official guidance recommends adjusting graphics and render scale if needed. Large-screen tablets automatically enable a tablet mode with a higher render scale; graphics and render scale remain adjustable. This is official guidance, not our independent device-by-device benchmark.

Xbox Cloud Gaming is separate

The PC and console FAQ also confirms Xbox Cloud Gaming through a Microsoft account at xbox.com/play on mobile, tablet, PC and Xbox. Cloud streaming is separate from the native iOS and Android app and does not confirm controller support, latency, regional availability or subscription terms.

What remains unconfirmed

The official notice does not publish a full iOS/Android model-by-model requirement table, a controller compatibility list, every graphics preset, regional store availability, or a complete device-performance matrix. Do not treat PC requirements, beta clips or third-party screenshots as mobile requirements.

Launch-day checklist

Use the same recoverable account and the same server as your existing character, update your device and store app, leave at least 18 GB free, use stable Wi-Fi, then check current official notices before troubleshooting. Record device model, OS version, game version, region and non-sensitive error wording before contacting support.

FAQ

When does Aniimo launch on mobile?

Official Aniimo support lists mobile launch as September 23, 2026 at 10:00 UTC+8: September 22 at 22:00 UTC-4 in America, September 23 at 02:00 UTC in Europe and September 23 at 10:00 UTC+8 in APAC.

Can I continue my PC character on mobile?

Yes on the same server. Official guidance says progress is fully shared across platforms on the same server; progress on different servers is separate.

Where can I download Aniimo mobile?

Use the official Google Play or App Store listing. Aniimo advises at least 18 GB free storage and stable Wi-Fi; avoid APK mirrors and unofficial clients.

Does Aniimo mobile support 60 FPS?

Official guidance says mobile supports 60 FPS except on a small number of lower-end devices. It also warns that 60 FPS can increase heat and recommends adjusting graphics and render scale when needed.

Will mobile support controllers?

A controller compatibility list is not provided in the official sources checked. Xbox Cloud Gaming is a separate streaming option and does not establish native-app controller support.
