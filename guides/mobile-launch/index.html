Aniimo Mobile Launch and Download Guide

Aniimo arrives on iOS and Android September 23, 2026. Use official stores, continue from PC with the same account, and avoid unverified mobile requirements.

Published2026-09-19Updated2026-09-20Information versionOfficial launch announcement / Xbox Cloud Gaming FAQInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-20

Mobile launch date

Pawprint Studio says Aniimo launches on iOS and Android on September 23, 2026. The official announcement does not publish a platform-specific unlock hour, so use the App Store, Google Play and official Aniimo channels for availability in your region.

Continue from PC with the same account

The official September 16 launch announcement says PC and mobile share the same data. If you created a character on PC, sign in on mobile with the same account to continue the same adventure. This confirms PC-to-mobile continuity; it does not publish a full purchase-sharing or server-transfer policy.

Use only official download routes

Google Play has an official Aniimo listing from Pawprint Studio; use that listing, the App Store, or a link from Aniimo’s official website. Do not install APKs, altered clients, key-download tools, account sellers or unofficial launchers. A store listing can vary by country or region and does not by itself establish that the native mobile client is playable before the announced September 23 launch date.

Xbox Cloud Gaming is a separate official option

The official PC and console FAQ says Aniimo supports Xbox Cloud Gaming through a Microsoft account at xbox.com/play, including streaming on mobile, tablet, PC and Xbox devices. Cloud streaming is not the same as the native iOS or Android app, and the announcement does not establish mobile device requirements, controller support, latency, regional availability or subscription conditions.

What is not yet confirmed

Official sources checked do not provide final iOS or Android device requirements, install size, supported controllers, graphics presets, frame-rate targets, region list, preload time or launch-hour table. Do not treat PC requirements, beta videos or third-party screenshots as mobile requirements.

Mobile launch-day checklist

Use the same recoverable account that holds your PC character, update your device and store app, check free space only after the official listing appears, read the current version notes, and expect possible login queues or slower loading at launch. Record device model, OS version and game version before reporting a problem.

Where to find verified updates

This guide will be updated when Aniimo publishes official mobile store links, device requirements or support notices. For PC requirements use the system-requirements guide; for same-server progress limits use the crossplay and cross-save guide.

FAQ

When does Aniimo launch on mobile?

Aniimo launches on iOS and Android September 23, 2026. The exact platform-specific unlock hour has not been published in the official launch announcement.

Can I continue my PC character on mobile?

Yes. Pawprint Studio says PC and mobile share the same data when you sign in with the same account.

Where can I download Aniimo mobile?

Use the App Store, Google Play, or links from the official Aniimo website. Avoid APK mirrors and unofficial clients.

What are Aniimo mobile requirements?

Final iOS and Android requirements have not been officially published in the sources checked.

Will mobile support controllers or high frame rates?

That has not been officially confirmed. We will not label it release-tested until it can be verified on the public mobile build.
