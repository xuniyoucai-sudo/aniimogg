Previous Playable record is not a current verification

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The October 4, 2026 review recorded Playable. This review did not re-confirm the current compatibility panel or its reasons. That evidence gap does not mean Valve has assigned its separate Unknown category, and it does not prove a rating change.

Playable and Verified are different categories

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Valve explains that Playable may require manual adjustments. Consult the current Steam compatibility details rather than treating either an old badge or a desktop specification as a performance guarantee.

PC requirements do not establish Deck performance

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The Steam store lists Windows 10, 8 GB RAM and 40 GB available storage as minimum requirements. These desktop requirements do not establish SteamOS compatibility, frame rate or battery life.

Safe troubleshooting — editorial checklist

Aniimo.GG editorial guidance

Record Deck model, SteamOS, Proton and game build, the exact error and the failing step. Check Steam’s current compatibility reasons, available updates and free storage. Current tested versions, FPS, battery life and a universal launch fix remain UNKNOWN.

Do not weaken security to troubleshoot

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

Do not disable anti-cheat or security protections, install unknown binaries, or assume Windows launcher instructions are verified SteamOS fixes. Use official support with a redacted reproduction record when the issue persists.

Cloud streaming is a separate route

Mixed sources; see evidence by section

The official PC/console FAQ describes Xbox Cloud Gaming. Cloud streaming is not evidence that the native Steam Deck client works. No native benchmark or account recovery was performed here.
