Aniimo Steam Deck Support

Aniimo Steam Deck support is not yet verified. Compare the official PC requirements and current compatibility status.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-01Information versionPre-launch official store checkInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-01

No official Steam Deck rating yet

Aniimo has not been marked Steam Deck Verified or Playable on its Steam store page. Pawprint Studio has not announced a dedicated handheld mode.

Why compatibility is uncertain

The minimum specification lists Windows 10, 12 GB RAM, an i7-9700 or Ryzen 5 3600X, and GTX 1060 or RX 6600-class graphics. These desktop requirements cannot be translated directly into guaranteed Deck performance.

Linux and anti-cheat

SteamOS compatibility can depend on Proton, launcher behavior, account services, codecs, and anti-cheat. Those details have not been confirmed for the release build.

Cloud gaming is not confirmed

Pawprint Studio has not announced Aniimo for GeForce NOW or another cloud-gaming catalogue. Its NVIDIA announcement concerns a post-launch NVIDIA ACE interaction feature, not cloud streaming. Do not treat general GeForce NOW support on Steam Deck as confirmation that Aniimo is playable there.

Testing plan

After release we will test installation, login, controls, text size, frame pacing, battery use, suspend/resume, and online modes before assigning a recommendation.

FAQ

Is Aniimo Steam Deck Verified?

No Steam Deck Verified or Playable rating is currently shown on the Steam store page.

Can Aniimo run on Steam Deck?

It has not been officially verified. Desktop PC requirements do not prove Steam Deck performance or SteamOS compatibility.

Will Aniimo be on GeForce NOW?

GeForce NOW support has not been officially announced. The current NVIDIA ACE announcement is about post-launch Aniimo interaction features, not cloud streaming.
