Aniimo Steam Deck Playable Status

Steam now rates Aniimo Playable on Steam Deck. See the official status, partial controller support and remaining handheld checks.

Official sources rechecked October 4, 2026. Current store and live-service details are reflected below; remaining unknowns stay explicitly marked.

Steam now rates Aniimo Playable

The current Steam store shows Aniimo as Playable on Steam Deck, not Verified. This replaces the earlier unknown status, but does not mean every interaction is optimized for handheld use.

Playable is not Verified

A Playable result can still involve manual setup, small text, launcher interaction or control limitations. Check Steam's detailed compatibility panel for the current reasons because public summaries vary by locale.

Use live requirements as a boundary

Steam now lists Windows 10, 8 GB RAM, GTX 970 or RX 5600-class graphics and 40 GB storage as minimum requirements. Desktop specifications do not predict Deck frame rate, battery life or every Proton update.

Controller support is partial

Steam lists partial controller support for Xbox and DualSense controllers. Keep touch, trackpads or a keyboard available for account, launcher or text-entry steps until the complete workflow is independently tested.

Cloud gaming is separate

Xbox Cloud Gaming is an official alternative on supported accounts and regions, but it is separate from native Steam Deck compatibility. GeForce NOW remains unconfirmed in the official sources checked.

Recheck after updates

Recheck login, text readability, input prompts, suspend and resume, online modes, frame pacing and battery use after client, Proton or compatibility updates. A Playable badge is not a performance guarantee.

FAQ

Is Aniimo Steam Deck Verified?

No. Steam currently rates it Playable rather than Verified.

Can Aniimo run on Steam Deck?

Steam's Playable category indicates it can be used, but some interactions may require extra steps and performance depends on settings and updates.

Does Aniimo have full controller support?

No. Steam lists partial support for Xbox and DualSense controllers.

Does Aniimo support cloud gaming?

Xbox Cloud Gaming is supported, but it is separate from the native Steam Deck rating.
