Is Aniimo Free to Play or a Gacha Game?

Aniimo is free to play with optional purchases. See what the official launch changed, what the live shop sells and why Beta gacha claims need context.

Last reviewed · 2026-09-11

Official sources rechecked October 4, 2026. Current store and live-service details are reflected below; remaining unknowns stay explicitly marked.

The base game is free

Official Steam and PlayStation listings describe Aniimo as free to play and disclose optional in-game purchases. A paid pack is not required to download the base client.

Why players still ask whether Aniimo is gacha

The final Beta used Rift Exploration for a low-chance Legendary encounter, so older articles and videos may call the game gacha. Pawprint Studio says the launch version removed repeated chance-based Irisalis attempts and replaced them with Legendary Tokens earned through exploration and story progress.

What the official launch route guarantees

Collect Legendary Tokens, craft a personal Legendary Aniipod, unlock the exclusive encounter, defeat Irisalis and use that Aniipod to seal the bond. Version 1.1 also adds a weekly-limited Iris Petal source and a separately crafted seasonal Aniipod with a stated 100% capture rate.

What the live shop appears to sell

A post-launch PC Gamer review records fixed-price cosmetics bought with Lumin Crystals and bundles containing items or currencies, with no character or equipment banner observed. This is an independent live-client observation, not a permanent promise about every future shop update.

Free does not mean every purchase is cosmetic

Official stores only confirm optional purchases; they do not classify the complete catalogue. Treat cosmetics, convenience items, materials, passes and limited bundles separately, and read the displayed contents and probability disclosure before paying.

A safe spending checklist

Ignore Beta price sheets, confirm the server and platform account, compare the exact bundle contents with earnable alternatives, check expiry and refund wording, and never buy through an unofficial top-up site.

What remains unconfirmed

Pawprint Studio has not made a universal promise that every future Aniimo or progression item will always avoid randomized acquisition. This page describes the current official Irisalis route and documented launch shop, not an irreversible lifetime guarantee.

Legendary acquisition changed for launch

Pawprint Studio replaced repeated chance-based Irisalis attempts with exploration and story tokens used to craft a personal Legendary Aniipod.

FAQ

Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes. The official stores list the base game as free to play with optional purchases.

Is Aniimo a gacha game at launch?

The cited launch client has no observed character or equipment banner, and the official Irisalis route uses earned tokens and crafted Aniipods. Older Beta gacha claims describe a system that Pawprint Studio says it redesigned.

Can I obtain Irisalis without paying?

The official route ties Legendary Tokens to world exploration and story progress. Follow the live event screen for current weekly limits and dates.

Are purchases only cosmetic?

Do not assume that. Fixed-price cosmetics are documented, but bundles may also contain items or currencies. Review each live offer separately.

Should I use a third-party top-up site?

No. Use only official platform and in-game payment channels to reduce account, refund and fraud risk.
