Is Aniimo Free to Play?

Aniimo is a free-to-play game with optional in-game purchases. Learn what is confirmed before launch.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-08-28Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Yes, the base game is free

Pawprint Studio and the official store listings describe Aniimo as free to play. The base client does not require a standard purchase price.

Optional purchases are confirmed

Steam and PlayStation both disclose optional in-game purchases. PlayStation also lists paid pre-order packs with cosmetics and Companion Handbook items.

What has not been fully detailed

Final prices, regional currencies, battle-pass cadence, premium currency rates, and the complete shop catalogue have not yet been published. Closed-beta values should not be treated as final.

Can you play without buying a pack?

Yes. PlayStation's store notice explicitly says the base game is available at no cost. Pre-order packs are optional extras, not an entry fee.

Our review standard

After launch we will separate cosmetic purchases, progression items, passes, and convenience features, then evaluate value without presenting spending as required.
