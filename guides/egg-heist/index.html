Aniimo Egg Heist Guide

How Operation: Egg Heist worked in the Aniimo beta, including teams, Lost Isles objectives, combat and launch caveats.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-02Information versionGlobal Closed Beta rule timelineInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-02

What Egg Heist is

Operation: Egg Heist is a three-player PvEvP activity in the Lost Isles. Beta notices describe gathering supplies, entering a forbidden area, fighting Alpha Aniimo or rival Pathfinders and competing for eggs including the Giant Darkler Egg.

Beta access and objectives

One beta preview required Veteran Pathfinder rank. A later update allowed evacuation after either personally submitting one Aniimo Egg or personally collecting a reduced Eggshell Coin target. These are dated beta rules, not guaranteed launch requirements.

Team egg sharing and evacuation risk

In the February 4 beta update, successfully submitted Aniimo Eggs were shared with the team after a safe evacuation. If evacuation failed, a player received only the Eggs they personally submitted. This makes individual submission and team survival distinct decisions.

PvP and Team Mode differences

The February 2 notes disabled carried-item rune effects in Operation: Egg Heist PvP while leaving them active in Egg Heist: Team Mode. The same update added carried items and runes to Alpha chest rewards and adjusted Eggshell Coin exchanges.

Protection and fixes

Beta patches added brief invulnerability when entering the battlefield and barriers near the central river, while also fixing minimap, matchmaking and unexpected untwine issues. These changes show the mode evolved quickly during testing.

Launch verification checklist

The public build must confirm unlock rank, queue types, team size, extraction targets, shared rewards, loss conditions, rune rules, shop limits, map flow and whether Darkler-form rewards remain available before this page is labeled release-tested.

FAQ

Is Egg Heist a three-player mode?

Official store and beta descriptions identify a three-player team, but the public launch queue must still be checked.

What is the main Egg Heist objective?

Beta versions involved gathering supplies and Eggs, confronting PvE or rival players, meeting a personal evacuation objective and extracting safely.

Are submitted Eggs shared with teammates?

A February 4 beta update shared successfully submitted Eggs after safe evacuation; failed evacuation returned only a player's own submitted Eggs.

Do carried-item runes work in PvP?

A February 2 beta update disabled rune effects in Operation: Egg Heist PvP while retaining them in Team Mode. Launch behavior is unconfirmed.

Are these final launch rules?

No. They are a sourced beta timeline and will be rechecked against the public version.
