Aniimo Egg Heist Guide

How Operation: Egg Heist worked in the Aniimo beta, including teams, Lost Isles objectives, combat and launch caveats.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-28Information versionGlobal Closed BetaInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

What Egg Heist is

Operation: Egg Heist is a PvEvP activity set in the Lost Isles. Official beta notes describe gathering supplies, entering a forbidden area, fighting other Pathfinders, and competing for a Giant Darkler Egg.

Team size and access

Official store text describes a three-player team. Beta patch notes required Veteran Pathfinder rank, but that unlock condition may change for the global release.

PvE and PvP choices

A beta announcement said players could explore and gather supplies or fight other teams. This suggests risk-versus-reward decisions rather than continuous forced combat, but final rules need release testing.

Beta-only details

Darkler-form rewards, matchmaking behavior, transport protection, scoring, extraction, and untwine fixes appeared in beta updates. None should be assumed unchanged at launch.

Launch guide plan

The release-tested version will cover objectives, map flow, team roles, loadouts, reward tables, loss conditions, matchmaking, and efficient routes.
