How to Change Aniimo and Set Up Your Team

The officially documented Beta ways to open Aniimo Management, switch Aniimo rosters and use Twine, plus what still needs public-build verification.

Published2026-09-10Updated2026-09-10Information versionOfficial Beta PC references — not release-testedInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-10

Short answer: the documented Beta entry points

The official Beta survival handbook says Esc opens the menus and Aniimo Management, while H switches between Aniimo rosters. These are useful navigation references, not confirmed release-version defaults or a complete team-management manual.

Open Aniimo Management

On the Beta PC reference, press Esc to open the menus and Aniimo Management. The handbook does not publish the menu layout, whether a party can be edited during combat, or any swapping limits, so this guide does not invent a loadout flow.

Switch between Aniimo rosters

The same handbook assigns H to switching Aniimo rosters. It confirms roster switching existed in the Beta, but not how many rosters, slots, presets, unlock conditions, cooldowns or multiplayer restrictions the public build will use.

Changing your active combat Aniimo is not documented

C is documented for using Twine with the selected combat Aniimo. That confirms a selected combat Aniimo, but it does not explain a separate active-member swap command. Wait for the release tutorial or official controls list before treating an assumed shortcut as final.

Build a safe launch-day team plan

Before committing materials, record your platform, game version and the on-screen names of the management and roster menus. Start flexibly and verify roles, elements, form conditions and swap rules in the public build. Avoid using Beta slot counts, costs or tier rankings for permanent decisions.

What we will test after launch

The release checklist covers menu paths, active-Aniimo swapping, roster size, presets, combat restrictions, controller and mobile controls, remapping, co-op party changes and platform progress. Confirmed results will be labelled with platform and version.

FAQ

How do I open Aniimo Management?

The official Beta handbook says Esc opens the menus and Aniimo Management on PC. The final menu path still needs release testing.

How do I switch Aniimo teams?

The Beta handbook says H switches Aniimo rosters. It does not confirm roster count, team size, presets or public-build restrictions.

Can I change my active Aniimo in combat?

The published Beta reference only says C uses Twine with the selected combat Aniimo. A distinct active-member swap command has not been officially documented.

Are these the final controls?

No. They are Beta PC references, not release-tested default controls. Console and mobile controls have not been published.
