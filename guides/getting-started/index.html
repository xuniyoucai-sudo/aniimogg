Aniimo Beginner Guide

A release-safe Aniimo beginner guide: platform choice, first-session checklist, account preparation, PC setup and launch-day facts that still need verification.

What is Aniimo?

Aniimo is a free-to-play creature-catching open-world RPG set on Idyll. Players catch creatures with Aniipods, Twine with collected Aniimo, and use their abilities for combat, puzzles and traversal such as gliding, diving and burrowing.

Choose a platform before creating a character

PC, consoles and Xbox cloud gaming launch September 16, 2026; iOS and Android follow September 23. Choose the platform you expect to use most before making a character: cross-save, shared purchases and platform-account linking are not yet confirmed.

A complete first-session order

Start by confirming the store build and current version, then choose a server only after seeing the live options. Check subtitle, audio, controller and accessibility settings before spending items or committing to a team. Play the opening guidance, record your platform and version, and treat early beta routes, values and reward tables as historical reference rather than launch instructions.

Account preparation and the ended web event

Use a recoverable email for platform and game-account prompts. Time to Aniimo ended September 3 at 23:59 UTC+8, so new players cannot join it now. Players who completed it should keep the same email available for the first character because the official event notice says its reward is delivered to that character.

Download, storage and accessibility

Steam lists 12 GB RAM and 45 GB available storage as its PC minimum. PS5 lists subtitles, basic remapping, sensitivity options, no-hold controls, manual saving, practice support and voice-chat transcription; those tags do not confirm identical settings on other platforms.

What not to decide on day one

Do not choose a permanent build, ranking, location route, purchase plan or cross-platform arrangement from beta-only information. Exact unlock hours outside the confirmed PS5 time, live server selection, account linking, download size, controller behaviour and cross-save rules need public-build verification.

Where to go next

Use the release-date guide for official timing, the system-requirements guide for PC checks, the catching guide for the confirmed capture flow, and the crossplay guide for the boundary between confirmed multiplayer support and unannounced account features.

FAQ

When does Aniimo launch?

PC, consoles and Xbox cloud gaming launch September 16, 2026; mobile follows September 23. PlayStation lists PS5 at 10:00 UTC+8, while other platform-specific unlock hours still need confirmation.

Do I need to pre-order Aniimo?

No. The base game is free to play. PlayStation pre-order packs are optional paid extras.

Can I still join the Time to Aniimo event?

No. Its official end time was September 3, 2026 at 23:59 UTC+8. Completed participants should keep the same email for their first launch character.

What should a new player check first?

Confirm the live version and server options, review settings, then follow the opening guidance. Do not use beta routes or values as final advice.

Can I move progress between platforms?

Cross-platform multiplayer is listed on Steam, but cross-save, account linking and shared purchases remain unconfirmed.
