Aniimo Beginner Guide

Aniimo release dates, platforms and beginner launch checklist.

What is Aniimo?

Aniimo is a free-to-play, next-generation creature-catching open-world RPG set on the continent of Idyll. Players catch creatures, Twine with them, and explore by gliding, diving and burrowing.

Launch dates

PC and console launch on September 16, 2026. Mobile launches on September 23, 2026.

Before you start

Pre-register through the official site, choose your launch platform, and treat closed-beta values as provisional until the release build is tested.
