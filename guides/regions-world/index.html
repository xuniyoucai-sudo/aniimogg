Aniimo Regions and World Guide

Aniimo's six officially revealed Idyll regions, what each area is known for, how to use the current map, and which locations still need release-build verification.

The continent of Idyll

Aniimo takes place across Idyll, an open world designed around catching, Twine abilities, traversal, challenges, races, discovery, and social camps.

The six regions confirmed so far

The official site currently names Forest of Falling Stars, Mistwoods, Echoback Landing, Sea of Flowers, Blitzwood and Nimbus Fields. This is a region index, not evidence that every point within each area is available, reachable or unchanged at launch.

Forest of Falling Stars and Mistwoods

Forest of Falling Stars is a dense purple forest where Celestises glide among starine trees. Mistwoods is a poisonous swamp forest where glowing fruit helps guide travelers.

Echoback Landing and Sea of Flowers

Echoback Landing centers on an enormous stranded Aniimo and the mystery of its song. Sea of Flowers surrounds a perfume-making village with vast purple flower fields.

Blitzwood and Nimbus Fields

Blitzwood is scarred by lightning drawn to Bolties and filled with thunder-struck trees. Nimbus Fields places low clouds over broad grasslands where Nimbis bounce across the terrain.

How to use the current Aniimo map

Use the map page to compare the six official region names and then return here for their world context. It deliberately does not claim coordinates, resource nodes, quests, fast-travel points or creature spawns. A useful release-tested map entry will identify its version, route, conditions and a reproducible location.

What we will verify after launch

The public build must confirm region boundaries, map labels, entrances, movement requirements, server or story gating, weather cycles, resources and spawn conditions. The September developer letter confirms a multi-area Home map, but it does not publish a field map or a coordinate list for Idyll.

Home expands for launch

The launch version adds a multi-area Home map, house building, furniture share codes, interactive furniture, improved Home Aniimo behavior, petting, discounted furniture resale, Prismana-trait eggs from RV dispatches and shared harvests from social tilling.

FAQ

How many Aniimo regions are officially revealed?

Six: Forest of Falling Stars, Mistwoods, Echoback Landing, Sea of Flowers, Blitzwood and Nimbus Fields.

Does the Aniimo map show creature locations?

Not yet. The current map intentionally limits itself to official region context until coordinates and spawns can be checked in the public build.

Are the six regions the complete launch map?

That has not been confirmed. The official site names these six regions but has not published a complete field-map boundary or coordinate list.

What is the difference between Idyll and Home?

Idyll is the adventure setting. The September developer letter separately confirms a multi-area Home map and Home features; it does not define Idyll's full field map.

Where can I find map updates?

Use the Aniimo Map page for the current scope, then check this guide for region context and version-aware updates.
