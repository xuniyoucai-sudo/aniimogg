Aniimo Homeland, RV and Idyll World Guide

A source-labelled Aniimo Homeland guide covering the RV, workers, farming, production, social features and the six officially named Idyll regions.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-10-02Information versionOfficial Home expansion / community public-build Homeland workflowInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-10-02

Homeland, Home and RV are related but different

The official developer letter confirms a multi-area Home map, house building, furniture share codes, interactive furniture and improved resident Aniimo behavior. Community public-build records use Homeland for the overall housing-production system, Home for the build space and RV for the mobile entrance and progression hub. Current client wording takes priority.

Start with the live tutorial, not a copied cost table

Community guides agree that Fannie and an RV quest introduce the system, but disagree on the consolidated unlock path and some limits. Follow the current quest log and RV menu; Aniimo.GG does not turn a disputed level, cap or cost into an official rule.

The safe production loop

A versioned public-build record describes the loop: place farmland or a facility, select a product, read its Ability Distribution, assign Aniimo that cover every required job, keep workers fed, and move output to storage. Cover missing jobs before chasing higher worker levels.

What the official launch changes confirm

Pawprint Studio confirms adjusted RV upgrade times, improved order refreshes, discounted furniture resale through the Co-op Store, RV dispatches that can produce Prismana-trait eggs, seasonal furniture and formulas, social tilling with shared harvests, and more responsive Home Aniimo interactions.

Friends and eggs in Home

Official material supports meeting other Pathfinders around RV camps and sharing harvests. Community public-build help also records visiting, soil help and Hatchinator assistance. Exact daily limits and time reductions must be checked in the live client.

The six officially named Idyll regions

The official site names Forest of Falling Stars, Mistwoods, Echoback Landing, Sea of Flowers, Blitzwood and Nimbus Fields. Homeland progression is separate from this field-region index; raising the RV does not itself unlock every region, spawn or story gate.

Map and farming claims need versions

The map page avoids unverified coordinates, resource nodes and spawn routes. Likewise, Homeland profit tables, crop yields, worker caps and refresh values should carry a client version and reproducible evidence before being treated as current.

A safe first-session checklist

Complete the live RV introduction, learn manual planting and collection, read each formula's required jobs, preserve enough combat Aniimo for the field team, stock worker food, collect output before it blocks production, and delay expensive layout optimization until the RV menu shows the real requirements.

Home expands for launch

The launch version adds a multi-area Home map, house building, furniture share codes, interactive furniture, improved Home Aniimo behavior, petting, discounted furniture resale, Prismana-trait eggs from RV dispatches and shared harvests from social tilling.

FAQ

What is Aniimo Homeland?

It is the housing and production system connected to the RV; official material confirms building, furniture, resident Aniimo, dispatch and social farming features.

How do I unlock Homeland?

Follow the current quest log and RV introduction involving Fannie. Community guides disagree on one consolidated gate, so this page does not invent an official level requirement.

How should I choose workers?

Read the formula's Ability Distribution and cover every required job before optimizing levels. Keep enough Aniimo available for the field team.

Do Homeland timers continue offline?

Community guides report offline behavior, but facilities can differ. Verify the timer and output cap shown in the current client.

How many Idyll regions are officially named?

Six: Forest of Falling Stars, Mistwoods, Echoback Landing, Sea of Flowers, Blitzwood and Nimbus Fields.
