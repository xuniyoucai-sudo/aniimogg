FAQ

Where should I download Aniimo now?

Use the official Aniimo site or the current Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation, Xbox, App Store or Google Play listing. Preload instructions are historical now that the game is live.

Can I reuse a Beta client?

No. The official launch guidance required the current launcher and client rather than an old test installation.

Is progress shared after installation?

Official guidance says progress is shared across platforms on the same server; different servers keep independent data.
