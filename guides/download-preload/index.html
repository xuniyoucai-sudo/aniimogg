Aniimo Download and Preload Guide

Where to download Aniimo and what is officially confirmed about preload on Steam, Epic, PS5, Xbox, iOS and Android.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-09-16Information versionOfficial PC and console pre-download FAQInformation statusOfficial information

Last reviewed · 2026-09-16

Official sources rechecked September 16, 2026. The newly published PC and console FAQ is reflected below; details the FAQ does not address remain explicitly marked.

Use official download links

The official September 14 FAQ provides a Windows PC launcher download and links to PlayStation 5 and Xbox pre-order pages. Use those official routes or the official Aniimo site; avoid unofficial APKs, launchers and key-download sites.

PC, PS5 and Xbox pre-download are confirmed

Pawprint Studio confirms pre-download for Windows PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox. PC users can download the official launcher; PS5 and Xbox users can pre-download after purchasing a platform pre-order pack. This does not confirm Epic Games Store or mobile pre-download.

Storage and installation

Reserve at least 40 GB of free space for the PC client and use an SSD where possible. The official FAQ asks console players to reserve 30–35 GB; the PS5 client installs only to the console’s internal SSD. Previous test clients cannot be used: install the latest launcher and client.

Launch time and account data

PC and console services open at September 16, 2026, 10:00 UTC+8. The same official FAQ says progress is shared across all platforms within the same server, while data on different servers remains independent.

What remains to verify

The official first-day patch notes, final installed size, Epic-specific preload, mobile download timing and exact in-game account-linking steps still need official confirmation or public-build verification.
