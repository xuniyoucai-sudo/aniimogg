Aniimo Download and Preload Guide

Where to download Aniimo and what is officially confirmed about preload on Steam, Epic, PS5, Xbox, iOS and Android.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-29Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-29

Where to download Aniimo

Use the platform links on the official Aniimo website for Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation 5, Xbox, App Store, and Google Play. Avoid unofficial APK, launcher, or key-download sites.

Is release preload available?

As checked on August 29, Pawprint Studio has not published a global-launch preload timetable. The July beta had a separate pre-download guide, but its dates and client do not confirm the release preload schedule.

PlayStation pre-order packs are not preload

PlayStation currently sells optional Advanced and Collector’s pre-order packs. The store states that the base game becomes free at launch; buying a reward pack does not prove that the release client can already be preloaded.

PC preparation

Steam lists 45 GB of available storage. Keep extra space for unpacking and patches, update GPU drivers, and sign in to the store account you plan to use before launch.

Console and mobile preparation

Enable automatic updates and verify free space on your device. Store pages can roll out at different times by region, so use the official listing rather than a search advertisement.

What we will update

This page will be updated when official preload times, launcher instructions, download sizes, and launch-day patch details are announced.
