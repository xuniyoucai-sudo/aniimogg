Aniimo Pre-Registration Guide

How to pre-register for Aniimo on PC, console and mobile, plus confirmed global milestone rewards.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-09-01Information versionPre-launch / Time to Aniimo eventInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-01

Where to pre-register

Start from the official Aniimo website and choose PS5, Xbox, Steam, Epic Games, App Store, or Google Play. Store availability can vary by country, so use the platform button shown for your region.

Official milestone rewards

The official campaign lists Glimmer ×200 at 10 million registrations, Aniipod Ultra ×2 at 15 million, the limited Companion Promise avatar frame at 20 million, and the Sunlit Meadow limited outfit at 30 million.

Time to Aniimo web event

The official Time to Aniimo event runs through September 3, 2026 at 23:59 UTC+8. Log in daily to hatch Aniimo, exchange duplicates with friends, and work toward the limited profile-card background reward.

Match the event email

The official notice says the event email must match the email used to create your first character after launch for the reward to be delivered. Use an address you control and do not create the first character with a different email if you want the event reward.

Event language scope

The web event currently supports English, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese. This four-language event page is separate from the full game's 13-language Steam listing.

Website registration and store wishlists

A website registration, store wishlist, mobile pre-order, and console pre-order are not necessarily the same action. Complete the official account registration and then follow the relevant store page for the platform you intend to use.

Paid PlayStation packs

Aniimo's base game is free. PlayStation sells optional pre-order packs containing cosmetics or a Companion Handbook; buying a pack is not required to play.
