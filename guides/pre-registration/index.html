Aniimo Pre-Registration Guide

How to pre-register for Aniimo on PC, console and mobile, plus confirmed global milestone rewards.

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-27

Where to pre-register

Start from the official Aniimo website and choose PS5, Xbox, Steam, Epic Games, App Store, or Google Play. Store availability can vary by country, so use the platform button shown for your region.

Official milestone rewards

The official campaign lists Glimmer ×200 at 10 million registrations, Aniipod Ultra ×2 at 15 million, the limited Companion Promise avatar frame at 20 million, and the Sunlit Meadow limited outfit at 30 million.

Website registration and store wishlists

A website registration, store wishlist, mobile pre-order, and console pre-order are not necessarily the same action. Complete the official account registration and then follow the relevant store page for the platform you intend to use.

Avoid account mismatch

Use an email and login method you can access at launch. Time-limited web events may require the event email to match the account used to create the first in-game character.

Paid PlayStation packs

Aniimo's base game is free. PlayStation may sell optional pre-order packs containing cosmetics or a Companion Handbook; buying a pack is not required to play.
