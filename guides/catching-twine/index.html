Catching and Twine System

How catching, Aniipods, traps, creature forms and the Twine transformation system work in Aniimo.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-09-03Information versionOfficial launch-version catching changes / Beta workflowInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-03

How catching works

Official descriptions say Pathfinders catch Aniimo with Aniipods, can set traps, and need to time the catch. Exact capture-rate formulas and item modifiers should be verified in the release build.

Irisalis has a redesigned legendary encounter

For launch, exploration and story progress award legendary tokens used to craft a personal Legendary Aniipod. Crafting it unlocks Irisalis's exclusive map encounter, described as a one-time battle and catch sequence.

Prismana, Lucky Strikes and Umbral encounters

Nurturing now has a flat base chance to trigger Prismana Flow while keeping its guarantee. Lucky Strikes can award items, add Prismatic Energy and directly trigger Prismana Flow. Wild Surges can produce Umbral Aniimo with Perfect Potential and distinctive coloration; exact rates remain unpublished.

Spawns respond to the world

Aniimo can appear according to environment, weather, phenomena, and time of day. A useful location guide therefore needs more than a single coordinate: it should record conditions and version.

What Twine means

Twine lets a Pathfinder become a collected Aniimo and use that creature's skills and perspective. It supports combat, puzzle solving, traversal, and environmental challenges.

Traversal examples

Official material specifically shows gliding, diving, and burrowing. Individual official Wiki entries also list mobility or pathfinding abilities, such as Emberpup's faster movement and Nimbi's aerial dash.

What we will verify at launch

We will test catch timing, trap behavior, failed-catch costs, form availability, stamina, cooldowns, and whether multiplayer changes capture ownership.

FAQ

How do you catch Aniimo?

Official descriptions say to use Aniipods, set traps and time the catch; the exact success formula is not published.

Do weather and time affect Aniimo spawns?

Official material says appearances can depend on environment, weather, phenomena and time of day.

What is Twine?

Twine lets a Pathfinder become a collected Aniimo and use that creature's abilities.

Can Twine help outside combat?

Yes. Official examples include puzzles, traversal, gliding, diving, burrowing and environmental challenges.

Are capture rates known?

No official numeric capture-rate table has been published, so beta impressions should not be treated as final.
