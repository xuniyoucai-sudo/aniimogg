Aniimo System Requirements

Official Aniimo PC requirements covering Windows, CPU, GPU, RAM, DirectX, internet and storage.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-08-28Information versionPre-launchInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Minimum PC requirements

64-bit Windows 10; Intel Core i7-9700 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600X; 12 GB RAM; NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 6600; DirectX 11; broadband internet; and 45 GB of available storage.

Recommended PC requirements

64-bit Windows 10 or newer; Intel Core i7-12700F or AMD Ryzen 7 7700X; 16 GB RAM; NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6800; DirectX 11; broadband internet; and 45 GB available storage.

Storage planning

The store lists 45 GB available space. Keep additional free space for unpacking, shader caches, patches, and screenshots rather than filling the drive to its limit.

Laptop and handheld notes

Mobile GPU names do not map directly to desktop cards. Steam Deck and other handheld compatibility have not been officially confirmed, so wait for launch-version testing before assuming support.

Internet requirement

Steam requires broadband internet and lists online interaction. Even if you focus on solo play, plan for downloads, account services, and online checks.
