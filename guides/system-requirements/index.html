Aniimo System Requirements

Official Aniimo PC requirements covering Windows, CPU, GPU, RAM, DirectX, internet and storage.

Published2026-08-27Updated2026-09-02Information versionPre-launch Steam listing / official DLSS announcementInformation statusOfficial information

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-09-02

Minimum PC requirements

64-bit Windows 10; Intel Core i7-9700 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600X; 12 GB RAM; NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 6600; DirectX 11; broadband internet; and 45 GB of available storage.

Recommended PC requirements

64-bit Windows 10 or newer; Intel Core i7-12700F or AMD Ryzen 7 7700X; 16 GB RAM; NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 8 GB or AMD Radeon RX 6800; DirectX 11; broadband internet; and 45 GB available storage.

DLSS 4.5 is officially announced

Pawprint Studio announced NVIDIA DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation and Super Resolution using a second-generation transformer model. The announcement describes dynamic frame multipliers intended to balance frame rate, image quality and responsiveness.

What DLSS support does not yet confirm

The official announcement does not provide a final supported-GPU table, launch preset list, latency measurements or per-resolution performance. AMD FSR, Intel XeSS, ray tracing and a frame-rate cap have not been officially confirmed in the sources checked.

Storage, laptop and handheld planning

Keep additional space beyond the listed 45 GB for unpacking, shader caches and patches. Mobile GPU names do not map directly to desktop cards, and Steam Deck compatibility remains unconfirmed.

Internet requirement

Steam requires broadband internet and lists online interaction. Even for solo play, plan for downloads, account services and online checks.

FAQ

How much storage does Aniimo need?

Steam currently lists 45 GB of available storage for both the minimum and recommended PC requirements.

Does Aniimo support DLSS 4.5?

Yes. Pawprint Studio officially announced DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation and Super Resolution; exact launch settings and supported GPUs still need confirmation.

Does Aniimo support FSR or XeSS?

AMD FSR and Intel XeSS have not been officially confirmed in the sources checked.

Does Aniimo require an SSD?

The Steam requirement table does not specify SSD or HDD. Keep extra room for unpacking and patches.

Can a laptop run Aniimo?

Compare its exact CPU and GPU with the listed desktop requirements. Mobile GPU names are not direct performance equivalents.

Is Aniimo Steam Deck compatible?

Steam Deck compatibility has not been officially confirmed. See the dedicated status guide.
