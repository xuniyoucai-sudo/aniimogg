Aniimo Elements and Roles

Official Aniimo elements, combat roles and beta type-counter information explained without speculative rankings.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-28Information versionOfficial Wiki / BetaInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-28

Nine official index elements

The official Wiki currently filters Aniimo by holy, fire, ice, dark, electric, grass, water, rock, and wind.

Five listed roles

The same index lists DPS, Heal, Support, BREAK, and REGEN. A creature may contribute through damage, survival, control, recovery, or breaking enemy defenses rather than raw attack alone.

Counters in beta

An official beta handbook mentioned fire, water, grass, and lightning counters and recommended a balanced roster for Alpha battles. The complete release matchup chart has not been published.

Dual-element and form changes

Official entries can show more than one element or different forms. Nimbi, for example, is listed with wind and electric variants, so one name does not always describe a single combat profile.

Building teams responsibly

Do not treat a role filter as a tier ranking. Release testing must account for skills, traits, forms, enemy weaknesses, stamina, and team synergy.
