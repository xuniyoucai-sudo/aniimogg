Aniimo Elements and Roles

How do Aniimo elements and roles work? See all nine official elements, five role labels and what is still unknown about counters and damage multipliers.

Published2026-08-28Updated2026-08-31Information versionOfficial Wiki / BetaInformation statusBeta — verify at launch

Written and reviewed by · Aniimo Guide Editorial Team · Last reviewed 2026-08-31

Nine official index elements

The official Wiki currently filters Aniimo by holy, fire, ice, dark, electric, grass, water, rock, and wind. These labels are confirmed; a complete numerical weakness chart is not.

Five listed roles

The same index lists DPS, Heal, Support, BREAK, and REGEN. A creature may contribute through damage, recovery, buffs or control, defense breaking, or energy recovery rather than raw attack alone.

Counters in beta

An official beta handbook mentioned fire, water, grass, and lightning counters and recommended a balanced roster for Alpha battles. The full release matchup chart and damage multipliers have not been published.

Dual-element and form changes

Official entries can show more than one element or different forms. Nimbi, for example, is listed with wind and electric variants, so one name does not always describe a single combat profile.

Building teams responsibly

Do not treat a role filter as a tier ranking. Release testing must account for skills, traits, forms, enemy weaknesses, stamina and team synergy.

FAQ

How many elements are in Aniimo?

The official Wiki currently lists nine: Holy, Fire, Ice, Dark, Electric, Grass, Water, Rock and Wind.

What roles are in Aniimo?

The official index uses DPS, Heal, Support, Break and Regen role labels.

What are the Aniimo element counters?

Official beta material mentioned counters involving Fire, Water, Grass and Electric, but the complete launch-version matchup chart and numerical multipliers have not been published.

Is an Aniimo role the same as a tier ranking?

No. A role describes a combat function, while a tier ranking requires release-version testing of skills, stats, forms and team synergy.
